# R26-LIFT-CEILING

## 2026-10-29 — INDEPENDENTLY_CHECKED

For fixed public simple base H and integer r≥2, a standard r-sheet lift with public fiber labels is exactly reconstructible by local simultaneous messages of at most Δ(H)⌈log₂ r⌉ bits per lifted vertex, with polynomial decoding and certainty. Each sends its neighbor's sheet index in every public adjacent fiber. [Proponent](../agents/lower_bounds/2026-10-29-analysis.md), [blind derivation](../agents/proof_auditor/2026-10-29-blind.md), [final audit](../agents/proof_auditor/2026-10-29-audit.md). For H=K_m,n=mr this gives O((n/r)log r). A sufficient ceiling to rule out Ω(n/log^C n) is r/log r=ω(log^C n). The proponent's loose wording was corrected after audit. This is not an unrestricted graph protocol.
